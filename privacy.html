Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

WhatsForDinner ("we", "our", "the app") is a meal planning and recipe discovery app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and name when you create an account (via Supabase authentication).
  • Preferences: Dietary restrictions, cuisine preferences, household size, and cooking skill level you provide during onboarding.
  • App usage data: Recipes you save, meal plans you create, shopping lists you generate, and order history. This data is stored locally on your device and in your Supabase account.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not track your browsing activity outside the app.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics SDKs.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Used for authentication and data storage. Their privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • Spoonacular API: Used to fetch recipe data. We send search queries (not personal data) to their API.
  • Kroger API: If you choose to connect your Kroger account, we use OAuth to authenticate you directly with Kroger. We do not store your Kroger password. Kroger's privacy policy: kroger.com/privacy
  • Grocery store links: When you tap to order from Instacart, Walmart, or Whole Foods/Amazon, we open their website or app. We do not share your data with them — you interact with those services directly.

Data Storage

Your recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Account data is synced via Supabase's hosted database. All network communication uses HTTPS encryption.

Your Rights

  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app's Profile settings.
  • You can export or request a copy of your data by contacting us.
  • You can use the app without creating an account (limited to local-only data).

Children's Privacy

WhatsForDinner is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.
